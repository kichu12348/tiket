# Tiket — Phase 1 competitor research

Research date: 20 October 2026. Scope: MakeMyPass, Luma (formerly commonly linked as lu.ma), Eventbrite and Tito Classic. This report accompanies [the Phase 2 audit and completion plan](</C:/VS Code/Projects/tiket/planning/2026-10-20-codebase-audit-and-roadmap.md>).

## Evidence and scope

The table describes documented product behavior, not a hands-on certification of competitors. MakeMyPass's public marketing and product update provide useful feature evidence, but less implementation detail than the other vendors' help centers. “Unverified” means the inspected public material did not settle the question; it does not mean the feature is absent. Plan, region, payment-method and product-version restrictions apply. Tito Pro is a distinct offering and is not silently combined with Tito Classic.

## Gaps in tiket

These are current-source findings, expanded and linked to code in the Phase 2 report. A screen, enum or table alone does not count as a completed feature.

1. **Payment correctness and inventory safety:** incomplete gateway verification/webhooks; no working expiring reservations; a mock paid endpoint remains reachable. Refund policy fields exist without a refund execution flow.
2. **Registration completion:** sign-in required, return-to-event broken after sign-in, only one ticket selected in the public UI, approval settings not enforced during order creation, required answers not validated there, file input stores a filename rather than uploading it.
3. **Commercial controls:** no coupon redemption, hidden-ticket access codes, bundle pricing, add-ons, affiliate commissions or campaign attribution model.
4. **Door operations:** API scan and manual check-in exist, but no camera scanner/offline synchronization/zone entitlement UI; admission and permission enforcement need repair.
5. **Organizer insight:** basic counts, revenue and exports exist; no live updates, conversion funnel, time-series sales/check-in analysis, abandoned checkout recovery or payment reconciliation workspace.
6. **Communication and documents:** email editor/templates/logs exist, but dispatch is a console/database placeholder. Document template CRUD and certificate/workflow tables do not issue certificates or execute automations.
7. **Platform readiness:** frontend type/lint failures, partial authorization, unsanitized public HTML, incomplete setup/migrations and no maintained automated regression suite.

## Features worth adopting

| Priority | Proposed feature | Product reason and benchmark |
| --- | --- | --- |
| P0 | One trusted payment/registration/inventory lifecycle | A confirmed pass must mean an eligible, completed registration; retry and outage behavior matter more than another settings screen. This is a tiket engineering requirement. |
| P1 | Short checkout, preserved return URL, per-attendee group data | Reduce friction; adopt the distinction between purchaser and attendee demonstrated by [Tito's flow](https://help.tito.io/en/articles/2002866-checkout-flow), with an account-light path inspired by [Luma](https://help.luma.com/p/event-registration-process). |
| P1 | Useful forms rather than more field widgets | Add ticket-specific/conditional questions only after server validation and uploads work; [Eventbrite's form controls](https://www.eventbrite.ca/help/en-ca/articles/228823/how-to-create-custom-questions-for-attendees/) are a concrete benchmark. |
| P1 | Coupons, access codes, group offers and add-ons | Build these on server-calculated, versioned price snapshots; [MakeMyPass's update](https://makemypass.substack.com/p/june-2025-updates-makemypass) establishes their competitive relevance. |
| P1 | Live operations view with financial reconciliation | Separate held, registered, paid, checked-in, cancelled and refunded numbers; show freshness and unresolved payments. Realtime reporting is documented by [Eventbrite](https://www.eventbrite.com/features/analytics/). |
| P1 | Reliable messaging and certificate delivery | Complete the existing email/document investment; reminders, delivery status and retryable jobs make it operationally useful. |
| P1 | Fast mobile scanner, then offline and zones | Validate scan speed, duplicate handling and staff privacy first. [MakeMyPass](https://app.makemypass.com/home) sets the offline/zone benchmark; [Tito](https://help.tito.io/en/articles/2006183-check-ins) demonstrates selectable check-in lists. |
| P2 | Attribution, recovery, integrations and engagement | Add campaign tracking, recovery and signed outbound webhooks; then validate demand for networking, booth visits, games and stage tools. Avoid claiming full ecosystem parity until those workflows are accepted. |

## Phase 1 assumptions and open questions

**Assumptions:** Initial focus is India-first community, college, conference and general-admission events using INR/Razorpay. “Seat” initially means a unit of admission capacity. This is a planning assumption, not a decision about assigned seating. Competitor claims are used as feature leads, not proof of reliability or speed.

**Open questions:** Is parity required for core ticketing/operations, or also MakeMyPass's broader engagement/stage/voting ecosystem? Are assigned seat maps, multi-day/session inventory, meal entitlements or on-site badge printers required for the first target customers? MakeMyPass's exact gateway, tax/refund policy, hold duration and integration contracts require a product demo or further public documentation before asserting detailed equivalence.
